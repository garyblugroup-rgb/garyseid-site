How this works

  1. Apply with one — or both

    Applying with two lenders lets you compare rates and fees. Multiple mortgage inquiries within a short window count as one for your credit score.

  2. Tell me when you've applied

    Call or text me at 314-494-6038 and I'll let them know to watch for your application, so nothing sits in a queue.

  3. We shop with your real number

    Once you're pre-approved, you know your true budget — and your offers carry weight with sellers.

What you'll want handy

Recent pay stubs, your last two W-2s or tax returns, two months of bank statements, and a photo ID. If you're self-employed, add two years of returns.
