Seller Guide · Marketing

How I market your home

Most homes in our market sell to first-time buyers — the people I work with every week. Marketing your home starts with knowing exactly who's going to buy it.

Priced from research, not a guess

Before your home goes anywhere near the market, I prepare a comparative market analysis the way an appraiser would: recent sales of similar homes, what's competing with you right now, and — just as important — what didn't sell and why. Price is one lever, not the whole plan. Position a home right and buyers compete for it; position it wrong and no price cut fully repairs the first impression.

Built for the first two weeks

A listing gets its most attention the first two weeks it's live — that's when every active buyer and every agent's saved search sees it. So everything is ready before launch: preparation done, professional photos taken, the write-up polished, the occupancy permit already started so your closing doesn't stall later. We launch once, and we launch right.

Everywhere buyers look

Your home goes on the MLS, which feeds Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com and every agent's search in both Missouri and Illinois — and because I'm licensed on both sides of the river, your listing works both markets from day one. I schedule the showings, collect feedback from every one, and report back to you so we always know how the market is responding.

I know your buyer

Here's the advantage that doesn't show up in a brochure: I spend every week with the buyers most likely to purchase your home. I know what they qualify for, which programs they're using, what makes them walk away after a showing, and what keeps their financing on track to closing day. When offers come in, I compare more than price — financing strength, contingencies, closing dates, and what the buyer is really asking you to pay for — and negotiate for the best overall deal, not just the biggest number.

Through closing, not just to contract

Accepted offers fall apart in the details: inspections, the appraisal, the buyer's loan, the occupancy permit. I manage each step and keep the deal moving, because a sale isn't marketed until it's closed.
