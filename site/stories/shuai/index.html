Client story · First-time buyers

New country, first house

Shuai and Linwei had never bought a home in the United States — and they were buying in one of the hottest seller's markets in years. In December 2022 they closed on their first home in St. Louis, with every contingency they needed intact.

First-time buyersSt. Louis, Missouri$275,000Closed December 2022

Renting didn't add up

Shuai and Linwei were doctors doing their residency at a St. Louis teaching hospital, and like a lot of residents, they ran the numbers and decided buying beat renting for the years they'd be here. One catch: they'd never bought a home in the United States, and the process here looks nothing like the process back home.

Explaining a system from scratch

Most first-time buyers need the process explained. Shuai and Linwei needed the whole system explained — earnest money, inspections, appraisals, title companies, none of it maps to how homes are bought in China. We toured house after house, often on same-day notice, and at each one I pointed out the flaws, not just the features, so they could build their own sense of what a house here is worth. A Chinese-speaking lender rounded out the team, so nothing got lost between the numbers and the paperwork.

Winning without gambling

They were buying when buyers all around them were waiving inspections and appraisals just to win. We talked honestly about what it takes to compete — and which risks a first-time buyer should never take just to get a yes. We lost a few houses to buyers willing to gamble more. Then we won the right one: in their price range, at a number they were comfortable with, keeping the contingencies they needed. We closed right through the Christmas and New Year holidays without missing a step.

The thank-you dinner

After closing, they insisted on taking me to dinner — a very authentic Chinese restaurant in University City, and I mean authentic. I let them order. I was born in the States, so my tastes lean more beef-and-broccoli than what showed up at that table, but I sampled everything and had a great time. I did politely decline the leftovers. I felt like I should be thanking them for letting me help, and they wouldn't hear of it.
