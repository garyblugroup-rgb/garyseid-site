Questions buyers ask

Frequently asked questions

Short, direct answers. If yours isn't here, ask me.

Do I need 20% down to buy my first home?

No. Many first-time buyers put down far less. FHA loans start at 3.5% down, some conventional loans at 3%, and USDA loans in eligible areas can require no down payment. A lender can tell you which programs fit your situation.

What does it cost to work with you?

Commissions are negotiable. Before we tour homes, we sign a buyer agreement that spells out exactly how I'm paid. In many deals the seller covers some or all of it, and I'll explain your options up front.

Which areas do you serve?

Both sides of the Mississippi: St. Louis City and St. Louis County in Missouri, and the Metro East in Illinois, including Edwardsville, Belleville, East St. Louis and Cahokia Heights. I'm licensed in both Missouri and Illinois.

Is buying in Missouri different from Illinois?

Yes. The contracts, disclosures and closing customs differ. Illinois has higher property taxes and a transfer tax, Missouri taxes vehicles yearly, and each state has its own radon and inspector rules. My First-Time Buyer Guide lays out every difference side by side.
