Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Gary Seid, REALTOR® · The Blu Group Real Estate, powered by Keller Williams Marquee

Information I collect

When you fill out a form on this site, I collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, and any property details or messages you include. The site also uses standard analytics and tracking tools (including a Follow Up Boss website tracker) that record pages visited and basic technical information such as browser type, in order to understand how visitors use the site and to serve you better when you reach out.

How I use it

Your information is used to respond to your inquiry, provide real estate services you request, and follow up about your home search or sale. It is stored in the customer relationship tools I use to run my business.

Data will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. I do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with service providers that help me operate my business (such as my CRM and website host), or as required to complete a transaction you are part of, or as required by law.

Calls and text messages

By submitting a form with your phone number, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Gary Seid about your inquiry. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing any property or service.

Your choices

You may ask me at any time to update or delete the information you have submitted, or to stop contacting you. Call or text 314-494-6038 or email gary@blustl.com.
