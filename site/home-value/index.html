Free home value analysis

What's your home really worth?

Online estimates guess from public records. I prepare a real comparative market analysis from recent MLS sales near you — free, written, and with no obligation to list.

Why not just use the Zestimate?

Online estimates can't see your house.

  • They can't see inside. Your updates, condition and layout never make it into an algorithm built on public records.
  • They can't see your street. A busy road, a great backyard or the block's best schools can swing a price by tens of thousands.
  • A CMA can. I compare your home to what actually sold nearby, the way an appraiser would, and give you a defensible price range plus an estimate of what you'd walk away with.
How it works

  1. Tell me about your home

    Send the form, or call or text 314-494-6038. A few details are enough to start.

  2. I pull the real comps

    Recent sales of similar homes near you from the MLS — not a formula.

  3. You get a written analysis

    A price range you can defend, what's competing with you right now, and an estimated net sheet. No cost, no obligation.
