Missouri vs. Illinois: what changes the payment

Illinois property taxes are higher than Missouri's, so the same-priced home usually carries a bigger monthly payment on the Illinois side — but Metro East homes often cost less to buy, and Missouri charges personal property tax on your vehicles while Illinois doesn't. That's why I tell buyers to compare full monthly payments, not list prices. I'm licensed in both states, so we can run the numbers on both sides of the river before you decide.
